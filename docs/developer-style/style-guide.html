04 / Developer implementation

Build the layout.
Don’t squeeze it in.

The meal and protein panels are the specific mobile revision in this handoff. The rest of the homepage, the new programme/product rows and the quiz are outside that change.

Separate regions: heading, image, description, doodle and button. The product image never sits behind the text.

01   Responsive geometry

Use normal document flow and CSS Grid. Mobile panels have 18–20px internal padding, 14px gaps and full-width actions. Desktop panels sit side by side from 1000px. No fixed card height, clipped copy, forced paragraph line breaks or overlapping artwork.

02   Buttons and form behaviour

One clear action per component. Use at least a 44 × 44 CSS-pixel target as the project standard; these card buttons are 50–52px high. Preserve existing quiz/form modals, visible close controls, keyboard focus and answer retention.

03   Content and images

Food images may crop within their own frame. Product packshots use object-fit: contain. The protein panel uses the existing tub asset, labelled “Packaging preview”; it does not invent final packaging, availability or claims.

04   Colour and accessibility

Use navy body copy and tested dark accents on pale panels. Normal text requires at least 4.5:1 contrast; large text 3:1. Never pale text on white. Labels, focus and errors must remain understandable without colour alone. [3–4]

05   Handoff and release

nutrition-fitness-tokens.css contains opt-in aliases, not a replacement for every stylesheet. The review component is home-feature-panels.css. Test from 320 to 1440px and with enlarged text. Review before merging; do not change production settings.
