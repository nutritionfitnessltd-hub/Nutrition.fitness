# Developer handover

## Preserve the approved system
Use the cream/aqua/blush/sage/petrol visual family, DM Sans and restrained handwritten accents. Jeff and Steff are illustrated AI characters; meals, workouts, families and products should otherwise use believable photography or controlled packaging mockups.

## Product rules
- Powders are unflavoured.
- Flavour shots are separate SKUs.
- Product-page flavour add-ons are one-off.
- Build-a-Box flavour shots recur only when explicitly selected with that physical cadence.
- One-off items must never silently become subscriptions.
- 4 weeks = 28 days; 8 weeks = 56 days; calendar month and annual are distinct.
- Show due today, delivery and every future charge before commitment.
- Do not allow conflicting monthly + annual NUFI access in one basket.

## NUFI data rules
- Targets originate from onboarding.
- Protein remaining is dominant.
- Logged food reconciles planned vs eaten.
- User edits are authoritative.
- Derived changes need explainable reasons.
- Wearable data should not automatically add wearable-estimated calories to food allowance.

## Production integrations
1. Catalogue/content
2. Identity and secured sessions
3. Orders/payments
4. Subscriptions
5. Fulfilment/webhooks
6. Courses/NUFI APIs
7. Support/messaging
8. Analytics/consent

Deploy through a preview branch first.
