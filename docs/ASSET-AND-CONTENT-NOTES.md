# Asset and content notes

The homepage visuals are reused from the user-supplied/approved design assets. Additional food-book crops are derived from supplied concept images. These are design assets, not evidence of photographed real customers, manufactured products or tested recipes. There are no fabricated customer ratings or testimonials in this rebuild.

Jeff and Steff use the approved supplied portraits and are explicitly labelled AI coaches. They must not be described as actual named human nutritionists, doctors or personal trainers.

Shop pouches, digital-book mock-ups and dropper bottles are constructed with HTML/CSS. They show the intended brand system and are labelled concepts. They are not supplier dielines, final pack artwork, verified formulation labels or ready-for-print food packaging. No unverified nutrition panel, clinical claim or serving dose has been fabricated.

Flavour names and the proposed 30 ml size do not establish allergen status, ingredients or final directions. Powders and shots need separate final product specifications. The proposed plant-based product is not represented as automatically allergen-free.

The six recipe pages are meal ideas for the site experience, not fully tested recipes with verified nutrition. Recipe book products have no PDF/ebook attached. Course pages show proposed curricula without invented playable video lessons. Programme schedules are labelled illustrative rather than personalised exercise prescriptions.

The source catalogue uses example commercial figures for demonstrating totals, not market research or approved launch pricing. Review `src/data.mjs` and the linked landing-page copy before publishing commercial offers.

The branding/registered-symbol treatment follows the user's provided designs; verify the correct trade mark usage and image rights in final production assets. No font files are distributed in this package.
