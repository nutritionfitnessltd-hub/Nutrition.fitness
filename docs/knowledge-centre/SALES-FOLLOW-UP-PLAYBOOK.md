# Nutrition.Fitness — sales follow-up playbook

Prepared 15 September 2026. This pack contains ready-to-personalise email and chat wording for all 50 Knowledge Centre articles. It is a sales resource, not an automated campaign, and no messages have been sent.

## The standard

Help a buyer make a decision they will still feel good about after paying. Answer the uncomfortable question before asking for the sale. Give the real cost, explain the work involved, make limitations visible, and say when a cheaper alternative or a different kind of help is better. A well-informed decision not to buy protects trust and reduces unsuitable customers and buyer’s remorse.

This follows the buyer-education approach associated with Marcus Sheridan’s [They Ask, You Answer](https://marcussheridan.com/they-ask-you-answer/). The operational standard here is our own: every article must resolve a real buyer uncertainty and have a clear use in a sales conversation. We judge its commercial contribution across the buying journey, not merely whether a reader purchases on the same page.

The often-quoted “30 pages / 80% close rate” story should not become a NUFI sales promise or a reading quota. Company case-study figures do not establish a universal close rate, an industry-wide benchmark or causation. People already intending to buy may also read more. We will measure our own results and send the few pages that answer this person’s question, not manufacture 30 page views.

## A useful follow-up has five parts

1. Name the concern the person actually raised. Do not invent a concern from demographic guesses.
2. Give a short, useful answer in the message itself. The article adds detail; it does not hold the answer hostage.
3. Send one relevant article. Add a second only if it answers the next obvious question.
4. Ask one discovery question that helps establish fit, budget, expectations or support needs.
5. Agree what happens next: a reply, a short conversation, reviewing options later, or no further contact.

Never send the whole library as homework. Never withhold an appointment because a person has not read a page. Offer a spoken explanation when that is easier or more accessible.

## A sensible sequence — adapt to the conversation

- First response: answer the question and send the single best article. Invite a reply about the unresolved point.
- Before a booked conversation: send up to two relevant pages, explain why they are useful and offer to cover them together instead.
- After the conversation: summarise their goal, what would be included, the total price and the main limitation. Send the one page addressing the remaining doubt.
- Before payment: confirm the actual offer, payment schedule, optional extras, renewal/cancellation/refund terms and whether the service is available. Check understanding, without pressuring someone to proceed.
- After purchase: set realistic expectations, point to the relevant getting-started answer, and provide a real route for help. This is where prevention of buyer’s remorse continues.

These are conversation stages, not a mandatory daily email schedule. Agree the next contact and stop promotional follow-ups when the person opts out. A reply, a purchase or a request for time changes the sequence.

## What protects the buyer and the business

**Be precise about the offer.** NUFI’s confirmed listed membership prices are £29.99 monthly and £99 annually. Twelve monthly payments total £359.88; £99 paid for a year is equivalent to £8.25 per month, not a monthly payment option. The large difference should be visible. Confirm inclusions, eligibility and terms before payment. Supplements and flavour shots are separate optional purchases. Do not invent free trials, cancellation guarantees, promotional deadlines or human-coaching hours. The current website still identifies checkout and member functionality as previews.

**Keep evidence behind claims.** Document the source and check date for prices and product comparisons; recheck volatile prices before using them in an individual quote. An independent clinical review has not been represented as completed. Use appropriately qualified review for material health advice as the centre develops. Do not let general educational information become personalised diagnosis, a promise of weight loss or a supplement treatment claim. The [CAP food and supplement rules](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/15.html) require authorised nutrition/health claims and their conditions of use; testimonials are not a substitute for evidence.

**Show the limits of a review.** The five reviews in this release use published product information. They are not hands-on tests. We do not invent taste scores, app-use experience, customer results or independent accreditation. Explain Nutrition.Fitness’s commercial interest, specify the criteria and let a competitor be the better option for a particular reader.

**Respect contact preferences.** For marketing email or electronic messages to individuals, establish appropriate consent or a qualifying soft opt-in before sending. An enquiry or page visit is not blanket permission for an ongoing campaign. The soft opt-in has conditions, including similar own products and opt-out opportunities at collection and in messages. Identify the sender, honour opt-outs, and screen future campaigns against suppression records. See [ICO electronic mail marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/electronic-mail-marketing/); it notes that guidance is under review. These templates do not supply consent or configure an unsubscribe system.

**Protect health information.** Do not put diagnoses, weigh-ins or sensitive concerns into tracking URLs, public notes or email subject lines. Keep conversation notes limited to what is needed, restrict access, and establish the appropriate data-protection basis before storing health information. Do not turn a vulnerable disclosure into a sales trigger.

## Measure whether this helps sales

Record the buyer’s stated concern, article sent, conversation stage, next agreed action, outcome and whether the choice stayed suitable after purchase. Start with manual CRM notes. No tracking or CRM integration has been added in this release.

Compare like-for-like cohorts over time: qualified enquiry-to-sale conversion, time to decision, objections still unanswered, returns/cancellations, and retention. Article-assisted sales are an association, not automatic proof the article caused the sale. Clicks and opens help diagnose use but do not demonstrate trust, comprehension or revenue. Avoid judging a page solely by traffic or sending extra articles just to hit an engagement target.

The highest-value client is someone the service can help, who understands the commitment and stays because it is useful. That is not necessarily the person buying the largest bundle on day one.
